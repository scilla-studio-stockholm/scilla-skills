# Business outcome format

A business outcome states a measurable change in the business in a fixed shape, so a team can tell whether it has been reached rather than argue about it. The shape forces a metric, a starting point, a destination, and a deadline into every outcome a team commits to.

## The template

`[Nå / Öka / Minska] [mätpunkt] från [nuläge] till [mål] till [tidsram]`

(Reach / Increase / Decrease a *metric* from a *baseline* to a *target* by a *deadline*.)

## Three rules

- **Mätbart (measurable)** — there is a number, and the team can see it move.
- **Effekt, inte funktion (effect, not feature)** — it names a result, not a thing to build. "Ship feature X" is not an outcome.
- **Tidsbestämt (time-bound)** — a deadline, so progress can be judged.

## Examples

- Öka månatligt aktiva användare i segment 18–35 från 15 000 till 25 000 till Q4 2026.
- Minska manuell hantering av kundärenden med 50 % till oktober 2026.
- Nå 60 % 7-dagars retention i SMB-segmentet till Q3 2025.

## Where it sits

A business outcome is the result the business wants. A team usually commits to a product outcome (a behaviour change it controls) that it believes moves the business outcome. See the product outcomes versus business outcomes reference.
