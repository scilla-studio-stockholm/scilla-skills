# The ambiguous ask

The asker said only "kan du hjälpa mig med intervjufrågorna?". Nothing says whether
they want questions written or questions reviewed.

- Asks which it is, or asks what they have in hand, before producing either. One
  question.
- Does not assume. Drafting a full interview guide, or reviewing questions that were
  never supplied, both fail.
- Short. Two to four sentences and the question.
- Answers in Swedish.

This is the case the merge in 0.5.0 was meant to fix. Before it, the same prompt reached
two different skills on different runs. The reply matters less than the consistency
recorded across runs.
