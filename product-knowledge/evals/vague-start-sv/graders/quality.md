# The vague entry

"We want to start talking to our customers but do not know where to begin."

- Asks one question that moves them forward: what they are trying to learn, what they
  are working on, or who the customers are. Not a menu of options to choose from.
- Does not deliver the whole method unprompted.
- May say in one sentence that the interviews get their direction from an outcome or a
  question the team needs answered, which is the honest next step, without turning into
  an outcome lecture.
- Answers in Swedish. Short.
