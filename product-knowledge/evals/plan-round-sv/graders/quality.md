# Planning a round

The first reply is the whole test here.

- **Produces no research questions and no interview guide yet.** This is the explicit
  rule in the skill: two or three sentences and one question, then wait. A first reply
  that hands over a finished one-page plan fails, however good the plan is.
- Asks for what it is missing: who is going to be interviewed, or what the team needs
  to learn to move the outcome. One question, not a list of four.
- Answers in Swedish.
- If it comments on the outcome, it does so in one sentence and moves on rather than
  turning the reply into an outcome review.
- No announcing sentence, no recap, no dashes as separators.
