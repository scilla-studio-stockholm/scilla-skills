# Teaching the method

- Answers in Swedish, because the asker wrote Swedish.
- Two to four sentences, then one question about what the asker is going to interview
  about. Not a lecture, not a numbered summary of the whole method.
- Says the core reason: people summarise and predict their own behaviour badly and
  reconstruct a specific past event well. An answer that describes the format without
  the reason has skipped the point of the method.
- Mentions the shape of the opening question, a specific past event ("berätta om
  senaste gången ...").
- Does not credit the method to a named author.
- No sentence announcing what follows, no praise of the question, no recap, no closing
  aphorism. No dashes used as separators.
