# Reviewing written questions

Four questions were given, each broken in a different way. A correct review takes them
one at a time and names the fault, and the four faults are distinguishable:

1. "Tycker du att vår onboarding är enkel?" — an opinion question, and leading, and
   answerable with yes or no.
2. "Skulle du använda en funktion som automatiskt fyller i dina uppgifter?" — a
   hypothetical about the future, and a solution pitch in the shape of a question.
3. "Vad brukar vara svårast när man kommer igång med ett nytt verktyg?" — asks for a
   generalisation ("brukar", "man"), not a specific past event.
4. "Varför valde du oss?" — a bare "why", which invites a rationalisation.

Required of the reply:

- One short paragraph per question, in complete sentences. Not a table, not a verdict
  list.
- Quotes the words that cause the problem rather than describing them abstractly.
- Gives a rewrite for each that asks for a specific past event, keeping the asker's own
  topic.
- Does not open a paragraph with a one-word verdict ("Svag.", "Dålig.", "Bra.").
- Answers in Swedish, and the rewritten questions are in Swedish, because that is the
  language they were written in.

Full marks require at least three of the four faults named correctly. Naming all four
as "leading questions" is a fail: the distinctions are the method.
